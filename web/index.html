Student Performance Analytics

Data Science & Visualization — Mini Project
1. Overview 2. Descriptive Analytics 3. Probability 4. Sampling & Estimation 5 & 7. Visualizations 6. Tabular Data
60 students · 4 semesters · 5 subjects
Built with Python + HTML5 Canvas + SVG + Google Charts

1. Overview

This project applies each stage of the analytics spectrum to one dataset: a batch of 60 students across four semesters and five subjects. The pipeline in analytics/analytics.py handles the data-science half; this page handles the visualization half.

2. Descriptive Analytics

Measures of central tendency, variation, and shape (skewness & kurtosis) computed per subject, per semester. Marks are a ratio-scale variable; gender and city are nominal.

Class average mark, by semester

3. Probability

Pass probability per subject (Bernoulli/PMF view), a Binomial model for "how many of 5 subjects will a student pass", and Bayes’ theorem relating city and pass outcome.

Pass probability per subject
Binomial PMF — subjects passed out of 5

4. Sampling & Estimation

500 random samples (size 15) were drawn from the population of semester-1 average marks to demonstrate the Central Limit Theorem, alongside Method-of-Moments and Maximum Likelihood estimates from a single sample.

Distribution of sample means (CLT demo)

5 & 7. Data Visualization

Charting primitives built directly on HTML5 Canvas and inline SVG — no charting library — plus a Google Chart for geographic distribution.
